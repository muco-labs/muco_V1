# Phase 8 — India & Tamil Nadu market audit

## National demand (summary)

India commercial search for MUCO’s core offers clusters around **service intent** (web/software/app/AI/SaaS/SEO), not city doorways. Buyers compare portfolio clarity, process, price transparency, and post-launch support. Tier-1 investment: national **service** and **industry solution** pages, `/india` and `/tamil-nadu` hubs, voluntary geo on leads—not per-city SEO pages.

## Competitors (India-wide)

National agencies and product studios compete on brand and case studies; regional players compete on price/packages. MUCO differentiates on integrated delivery (web + software + CRM stack), honest labelling of concept work, and founder-led engineering—not fake scale claims.
