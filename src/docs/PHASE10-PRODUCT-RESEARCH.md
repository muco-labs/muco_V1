## Primary opportunity

**MUCO Client Hub** (`client-hub`) — productized client workspace (projects, files, invoices, messages, support) for agencies and professional services.

## Secondary

**Lead follow-up layer** — optional add-on aligned with existing CRM; only after Client Hub validation.

## Future

Vertical control-plane concepts (portfolio), commerce headless patterns, operations intelligence — remain **concept** until a specific ICP validates.

| Criterion | Status |
