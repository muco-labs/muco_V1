# Ethical review request workflow

**Goal:** Honest Google/third-party reviews from customers after real delivery.

## When to ask

- Project successfully handed off and support window explained  
- Customer has used the deliverable for at least 2 weeks (or agreed milestone)  
- You have permission to use their company name in testimonials separately (optional)

## How to ask

1. Personal email or call from the project lead—not a mail merge blast.  
2. Explain that honest feedback helps other Erode/TN businesses find reliable partners.  
3. Share a direct link to your **verified** Google review surface (from GBP).  
4. Accept neutral or negative feedback; offer to resolve issues before they review.

## Do not

- Offer discounts or gifts tied to star rating  
- Provide scripted review text  
- Review your own business or ask employees to pose as customers  
- Incentivize only 5-star reviews  

## Internal tracking

- Log request date and outcome in CRM lead/customer notes (no public display).  
- If testimonial text is provided for the website, store permission in CRM/files per Phase 6 testimonial rules.
